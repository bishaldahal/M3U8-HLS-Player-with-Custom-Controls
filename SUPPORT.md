# Getting help

- **Bug?** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/bishaldahal/M3U8-HLS-Player-with-Custom-Controls/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Idea?** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/bishaldahal/M3U8-HLS-Player-with-Custom-Controls/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Question?** Use [Discussions](https://github.com/bishaldahal/M3U8-HLS-Player-with-Custom-Controls/discussions).
- **Security issue?** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The extension can't bypass DRM or play streams that your browser can't access, such as geo-blocked or expired-token URLs.
